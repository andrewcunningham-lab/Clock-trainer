Working the switch

  • The switch springs back to center. The shot clock stays frozen at the reset number as long as you hold it.
  • It only counts down while the main clock runs.
  • Made basket: hold right through the inbound. Let go when the pass is touched.
  • Steal or defensive rebound, live ball: push right, let go right away.
  • Foul or violation: push right and let go. It waits for the main clock.
  • Shot hits the rim: hold right until a team gains control, then let go.
  • Kicked or fisted ball under 20: push left.

Shot clock calls

NFHS model rule used by the IHSA · 35 seconds

Let go of the switch

  • On a throw-in, when the ball is legally touched inbounds
  • When a team gains control (rebound, loose ball, jump ball tap)

Push right · 35

  • Change of team control
  • A single personal foul
  • A single technical foul
  • A try that hits the ring or flange

Push left · 20

  • Defense intentionally kicks or fists the ball with less than 20 on the shot clock

Don't touch it

  • Defense knocks it out of bounds, a timeout with the offense keeping the ball, or any other stoppage that isn't on this card
  • Shot clock hits zero: the horn sounds and the officials rule

Shot clock goes dark

  • When the game clock shows less than the shot clock. Your box can do this on its own (the ST > MT Blank option); otherwise the main clock operator uses Set, Shot Time, Blank.

Quick reference built from the NFHS model rule the IHSA adopted. The 35-second shot clock is required in Illinois varsity games starting this 2026-27 season. Check the IHSA's current shot clock operator guidance before you use this card to certify anyone. Your box's short reset should read 20. If it shows 15, change "Set OB Time" in the shot clock options on the MPC-7.
